# Nominations and Newsletter: in short

The main changes in the TOURISE 2027 admin, 12 October 2026. Details and checks: "Nominations and Newsletter update".

## 1. Two new email statuses

Both sections now follow each email as Mailtrap reports it: **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Opened**, **Clicked**, and two new ones. Under each new one, a short line gives the receiving server's reason.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Delivery delayed** | The recipient's server asked Mailtrap to try later. Mailtrap keeps retrying; it may still arrive. |
| **Not delivered** | The email will not arrive: the address or domain does not exist, or the server refused it. |

## 2. Nominations

- **CC and BCC for each nominee**, from two new Excel columns just before Language. Several addresses per cell, separated by commas; never the nominee's own address.
- **Not delivered and Delivery delayed** on the batch page, the batches list, the dashboard, a nominee's email history and the export, each with its reason.
- **The 48 reminder emails of 11 October** that never arrived are now marked Not delivered, with their reasons. Most are domains or mailboxes that do not exist.
- **A steadier batch page:** a selection bar (Select all matching, Send to selected), the Language filter no longer hides Arabic nominees, and no more endless refresh.

## 3. Newsletter

- **Import subscribers from Excel:** source Imported, subscribed, 1,000 per file. An address already in the newsletter refuses the file.
- **Real tracking:** delivery, opens, clicks, Not delivered and Delivery delayed reported by Mailtrap, and a **page for each campaign** listing every recipient with their status and reason.
- **Settings tab** to choose the newsletter's sending account; **Search** on Subscribers; **Export** on Campaigns; **one-click unsubscribe** in Gmail and Yahoo.
- **Access tab by tab:** 13 role boxes, one to see each tab and one for each button.

> **What you need to do:** if you manage roles, tick the new Newsletter boxes on every role that uses the section. Until then only Super Admins see the Newsletter.
