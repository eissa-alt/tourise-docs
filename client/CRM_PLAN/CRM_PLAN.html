Prospects · CRM

CRM Plan

A new Prospects section in the TOURISE 2027 admin: the people we may invite, where each one is in the system, and inviting them. Revised for the client's template v4.1. Plan only, nothing built yet. 19 October 2026.

1. In short

The team keeps lists of people it may want at the event in Excel, and the sheet cannot tell whether a person has asked for an invitation, been invited, or registered. The Prospects section imports the client's CRM sheet (TOURISE_CRM_Fields_and_Import_Template_v4.1.xlsx) and adds what Excel cannot:

  • Tracking, the heart of it: for every person, where they are in the system (in CRM, requested, invited, sent, opened, registered, confirmed), with dates and a link to each record (part 3).
  • Sub-categories across the whole system: defined in Category settings, chosen with the category, carried as a tag on guests, invitations and the CRM (part 4).
  • Owners who are admins, notified of their reminders and of their people's progress, with Prospects set up in Roles and limited by category (part 5).
  • The template's Invitation Channel, Security Clearance and Invitation Status, with their rules enforced (part 6).
  • Tabs per category, search and filters, Invite to a collection, a report by owner, export, and who did what on every action.

Decisions needed: nine points, listed with a space for the answer in the last part, Questions. Four of them need the client. Everything else is decided.

  • On the list: a Tracking column with the furthest step and its date, and the steps as dots, so a glance shows how far each person got. Filters such as invited, not opened after 7 days or sent, not registered.
  • On the person's page: the journey as a timeline at the top, each step opening its request, invitation or guest; then the team's activities and the history.

  • Each move notifies the owner (part 5).

One in use is switched off, never deleted: records keep it, it is no longer offered. Seeded from the client's Badge Categories sheet (about 40, e.g. Dignitary: Head of State International, Ministers & Excellencies International, Ambassadors).

  • Changing the category clears the sub-category and asks for the new one in the same step.

5. Owners, roles and notifications

  • The Relationship Owner and the Follow-up Owner are admin accounts with the Prospects permission for the person's category. The picker offers only those admins. A blank Follow-up Owner means the Relationship Owner.
  • Set up in Roles, with the other modules: View, Create, Update, Delete, Invite, Export, Settings, Record History, Audit Trail, View tier and Can own prospects.
  • Per category: the role's category limits apply everywhere. An admin limited to Speakers sees, imports, owns, invites, reports on and exports Speakers only. Moving a person to a category their owner cannot see asks for a new owner.

OptionWhat happensTrade-off
A. Accepted = registered RECOMMENDEDThe invitation stays the registration link; the status reads Accepted once they register, Declined when they say no.No new step for the guest; the client's two steps become one.
B. Two stepsThe invitation asks accept or decline first; Accepted sends a second email with the link.Follows the wording; a new page and email, and every guest clicks twice.

Part B · For developers

11. Approach

  • Tracking is computed, never stored: through the link first (prospects.invitation_id → invitation → guests.code_id), then by lower-cased email over guests, invitations and requests; furthest step wins; filtered in SQL with EXISTS. A stored copy would need updating from four modules.
  • Sub-categories: sub_categories (category_id, name_en, name_ar, sort_order, is_active) and a nullable sub_category_id on guests, invitations, invitation_collections, prospects; the server checks it belongs to the record's category. The guest's three save paths (public store, admin create, admin update) each take it, tested through HTTP.
  • Inviting goes through Invitations: the body of addGuests() moves into a service both call; invitations made here carry created_via = 'prospect'.
  • Owners: relationship_owner_id and follow_up_owner_id are admin ids; reassignments are activity entries. A daily scheduled job raises due and overdue reminders; tracking moves notify through AdminActivityFeed.
  • Roles: a prospects feature in AdminPermissions, with the role's category limits applied to every query.
  • The template is generated by the system with working dropdowns (dependent Category → Sub-category, Industry → Sub-industry); columns read by heading, so the client's file still parses.

13. Out of scope

  • Matching by mobile or name for tracking (name only flags a possible duplicate); merging, unless 7.4 B.
  • The Tier Sign-off approval inside the system; sub-event invitations; emailing prospects without inviting them (that is Nominations).
  • Any public route except registration taking the invitation's sub-category; the mobile app.
